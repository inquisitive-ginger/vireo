# 2026-07-30 — fix session: auto-bug #92 (ongoing chore respawns immediately)

Ad-hoc fix session per `docs/fix-it-playbook.md`; no kickoff existed, none written
(single-issue triage-to-merge, this log is the artifact).

## The report

Zoë (tier 3, report `e7374f56`, issue #92): completing an ongoing chore made the
next occurrence appear immediately — completed + fresh pending both in the list,
double P$. Filed 04:51 UTC, i.e. right around the local midnight rollover.

## Root cause

ADR 058's same-day-respawn guard is the `(routineId, dueDate)` unique index, and
it assumes the completed row is dated _today_. But the roll-to-today for an open
ongoing row only runs inside `reconcileRoutines` — on read. On an always-on
client (the fridge iPad) the list goes stale across midnight, so the completion
lands on a row still dated yesterday. The post-mutation refetch reconciles with
today's key, finds no open row, and the unique on `(routineId, yesterday)` does
nothing to stop spawning `(routineId, today)` — a fresh pending row, instantly.

## Fix (PR #93, merged `f1b5ab0`)

`PATCH /chores/tasks/:id/complete` now takes the client's local `?today=` (same
convention as the GET) and, for an ongoing routine row dated earlier, re-dates it
to today as part of completing — the completion owns the day's slot and the
respawn hits the unique index. Roll forward only; P2002 falls back to completing
without the re-date; clients that omit the param keep old behavior. `days`
routines are untouched (their next spawn is a future scheduled day, visible ahead
by design). Regression test reproduces the stale-completion sequence and fails
without the fix; full API suite (392) and chores plugin suite (88) green.

## Follow-up outage (2026-07-31, PR #95, merged `22ab2df`)

Right after #93 deployed, chores went down in prod — GET /chores/tasks 500 for
everyone, widget empty. The #92 incident's data was a landmine: a pending row
dated 07-30 sat behind a completed row owning 07-31, and reconcile's roll-forward
tried to re-date the pending row onto the taken `(routineId, dueDate)` key — the
unguarded `task.update` threw P2002, aborting the whole read. The spawn was
guarded, the roll never was ("rolls are idempotent updates" only held while the
target key was empty). The roll now skips on unique violation and the row rolls
once the key frees up. Verified live: endpoint back to 200, zero 500s post-deploy.

## Corrected root cause (2026-07-31, PR #96, merged `75cd36b`)

Zach's timeline (completion 5–10pm PDT 7/30, no clawback — the log's earlier
reopen story was wrong, and the "stale row" mechanism in #93 was only half the
picture) pins the real sequence. Evening PDT is already the next day UTC; a read
without `?today=` — the fallback path, reachable by any stale pre-#90 tab and
even unauthenticated (no session ⇒ family pseudo-profile) — reconciled with the
server's UTC date and rolled the open Bedtime row ahead to 07-31. Zoë completed
that rolled-ahead row at 9:48pm (#93's re-date is forward-only, so its future
date stood); her refetch with `today=07-30` found no open row, and the unique on
07-31 didn't block spawning 07-30 — the immediate respawn. Next morning every
client reached `today=07-31` and the roll onto the taken key was the 500.

Three closures in #96: a read without `?today=` serves but never materializes;
an ongoing completion re-dates the row to the completer's local day in **both**
directions (an ongoing row's dueDate only means "which day's completion is
this"); an ongoing spawn skips when any row is dated today-or-later. Tests
replay the exact evening and fail without the fix.
