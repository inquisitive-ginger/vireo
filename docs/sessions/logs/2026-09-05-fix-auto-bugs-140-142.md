## #140 — the XP plate was missing from Punch cards

**Reported by Zoë (tier 3), `/chores`.**

The plate belongs to the taskee surfaces, and M4/6 split the kid's page into **Today** and
**Punch cards** — the tally tab is neither, so it kept a `PageHeader` with a title and
nothing else. `ChoresPageView` now computes the lifetime seed once and hands it to both
halves.

Wiring it turned up the reason it could not simply be passed through:

**`chores/xp` was never invalidated on a completion.** `useXpFlight` keeps Today's plate
current locally and deliberately does not re-seed from later fetches, so nothing noticed
the query going stale. But `PunchCardsTab` has no flight of its own, and switching tabs
unmounts the taskee surface — so the plate read the seed, which was the total _before_ the
session's completions, and switching back reset Today's plate to it too. `completeTask`
invalidates the key now. **This was a live bug before the tab ever gained a plate.**

The plate also now shows on a kid's **empty** Today, which had a bare title. Same header,
both tabs, both states.

## #141 — no way to cancel out of editing a shopping row

**Reported by Dad (tier 4), `/shopping/lists`, 1112×814.**

The report named the rename field. The panel it is reached through had the same defect and
a worse version of it, so both are fixed.

**The rename field** exited on Escape, Enter or blur — and Enter and blur both commit.
Escape was the only cancel, on a surface with no keyboard. It gets a × beside the field.
The mechanism is the interesting part: the blur the × itself causes is the one that used
to commit, so `pointerdown` sets the cancel mark and that blur _becomes_ the cancel. A
pointer that goes down on × and lifts elsewhere never fires a click, and a field left
focusless in rename mode would be the same dead end again.

**The inline actions panel** (tablet and up) had _only_ Escape, and three verbs that all
do something. It gets a × in its header row. **Inline only** — the phone's bottom sheet
dismisses by scrim, grab bar and Escape already, and this is now the one place the two
presentations differ. `RowActionsSheet.tsx` says why.
