The grocery list — every decision, drawn

This is the single visual the build reads, alongside 2026-08-23-grocery-list-handoff.md. The spec says what is decided; this says what it looks like. Where they disagree, this file is the newer of the two — it carries the index decision the original gate never made.

New since the handoff

The index is option B — lists lead the page, and every list row carries its own add affordance so an item can be filed without opening its list. The staple chip strip is cut from both surfaces, and ghost completion sources from previous entries — the distinct item names this family has added before, across all its lists, most recent first. It matches a fixed staple seed today (GroceryAddBar.tsx:60); that seed becomes the history's initial contents so completion works on day one, and the separate Staple concept leaves the UI entirely.

Unchanged

Row content, aisle grouping, permanent-vs-runtime lists, tier 4 to create, and the wall's structure are all as the handoff settled them. They are redrawn here so nothing has to be inferred.

04Spec

Do not reintroduce

Each was considered and rejected with a reason. Rebuilding one is rework, not initiative.

× One list grouped by store Can't tell you what a trip contains — you'd see Costco while standing in the grocery store.
× Tabs, or a segmented pill switcher, across the top A fourth list overflows 390px, a list that appears and vanishes makes the strip jump between visits, and it is a fourth navigation pattern beside the sidebar, tab bar, and drawers.
× A staple chip strip on either surface Cut 2026-08-23. What the family has added before is the completion dictionary; a fixed staple list is not.
× A suggestion sheet or a labelled pill strip while typing Both move layout mid-keystroke; inline completion does not.
× A note line on the row — purpose, provenance, or "Sam got it" Aisle grouping guarantees a meal's items are never adjacent, so the note repeats. Supersedes design.md AC6/AC7.
× A who-added dot, or any identity chrome on a grocery row On a two-person list the answer is always one of two people (ADR 064).
× Columns on the wall Costs aisle heads at four feet and makes the wall group differently from the phone.
× A store picker inside a list The destination is already implied by the list you are standing in.
05Spec

Component mapping

Nothing here is hand-rolled. If a surface seems to need a new primitive, that is a question for the user, not a decision for the build.

SurfaceBuild from
List index rowItemCard — owns the row skeleton and onActivate. The add affordance is a second region inside the same card, divided by a hairline.
Grocery item rowListRow — leading = check ring, children = name + right-aligned qty in the min-w-0 flex-1 wrapper. No prop-shape change.
Aisle group headtext-section-label, sticky within the scroll context
Page framePageContainer width="list" — a list is not prose; padding is not a prop
Composer + completionGenuinely new — no existing primitive covers a persistent composer or the inline-completion input
"New list" rowDashed ghost in the ItemCard rhythm; becomes the field in place. Tier 4 renders it, tiers 1–3 never do
