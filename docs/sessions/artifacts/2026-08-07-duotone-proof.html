1 · The mechanism race — one hue, three sizes, light and dark

Canvas LUT interpolates between band targets in OKLCh (hue held to the owner's). The SVG filter is feColorMatrix(saturate 0) → feComponentTransfer type="table" (33 samples of the same LUT, color-interpolation-filters="sRGB"). CSS is the classic blend/mask duotone — it has no third stop, and in dark theme it can only darken, while the dark triplet needs line and feature to come out lighter than the fill. Expect it to lose there.
