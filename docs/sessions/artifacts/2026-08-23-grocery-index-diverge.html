The grocery index

The gate on 2026-08-23 designed the row — what it carries, what happens while you type, whether the wall gets columns. It settled per-store lists as a consequence of that work, in prose, and never drew the surface those lists live on. Two build passes have now produced the same page: the old composer with an index bolted under it. That is not a build failure. There was nothing to build from.

Does the home surface route, or capture?

Every option below answers that differently, and the answer decides everything else — where the composer lives, whether an index is a page at all, and what the switcher has to be. Two permanent lists plus the occasional runtime one means this page is usually two to four rows. That is the constraint the options are arguing with.

The four

Phone at 390px — the surface that decides. Real tokens, real type scale.

Home is the lists and nothing else. Adding happens inside a list, where the destination is already implied. The structure is the whole page.

Costs: a tap before every add — the most frequent action on the module.

What the last two passes actually shipped

For comparison, not as an option. Both are A with a composer stapled on — pass one put it above the lists with a ten-chip staple strip, pass two moved it below and cut the strip. Neither is a position on the axis above, because the axis was never put to anyone.

The switcher is the tell. Pass two shrank the store picker to a small dropdown beside the field. That is the right instinct under option A — the picker is vestigial there, since a list you are standing in implies its own destination — and the wrong shape under C, where the switcher is the primary navigation of the whole module and should be the largest thing on the page.

A picker sized like a form control means the surface has not decided what it is.

What I need from you

  1. A, B, or C. They do not compose — each answers the axis differently.
  2. If C: does the wall tablet follow it, or does the wall stay an index? The gate settled "same index → drill-in as the phone," which C dissolves.
  3. Staples. The last pass cut the ten-chip strip from the index on its own judgement. Under A and B it has nowhere to live; under C it sits with the composer. Confirm cut, or confirm it belongs inside a list only.

Then I compose the final dev-ready artifact — this decision plus the row, typing, and wall decisions already settled — as the single visual the build reads alongside the spec.
