# Brief

The living synthesis of everything decided. Vireo's synthesis already lives in three documents; sessions read the sections they touch.

- **What it is, for whom, what it isn't:** `docs/idea.md`.
- **Look, type, colour, components, identity and status colour:** `docs/design-system.md`. Design decisions lock there (D-012, D-044, D-065, D-074).
- **How the pieces fit:** `docs/architecture.md`. Cross-cutting technical decisions lock there and as ADRs.
- **Running and deploying it:** `docs/deployment.md`.

## Designing for Vireo

Design for the situation, not the viewport: the wall is read at arm's length, glanced at rather than read, often with wet hands; phones are one-handed. In a decide session, name the axis the options disagree on, offer three, and let one break a house convention and say which. Diverge in plain HTML with the real tokens from `packages/ui/src/styles.css`, then render the survivors in the real app beside what already exists, where redundancy shows. Storybook is where a decision is delivered, never where it's made.
