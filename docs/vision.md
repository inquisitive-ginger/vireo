# Family OS — Vision

## Name

"Family OS" is a working title. Rename globally when the real name lands.

## What It Is

A locally-hosted ecosystem of applications and integrations that helps
a family organize, coordinate, and grow together. Not a product — a
platform that evolves with the family that runs it.

## The Problem It Solves

Family life is managed across a fragmented set of apps, tools, and
mental overhead. Chores, schedules, finances, routines, and
communication all live in different places with no shared context.
Family OS brings these into a single coherent system — on your
hardware, under your control.

## Core Beliefs

- **Kids are participants, not subjects.** The system gives children
  real agency appropriate to their age and trust level.
- **The system should grow up with the family.** Features, complexity,
  and autonomy expand as kids get older.
- **Local-first, always.** Runs on a home server. Your family's data
  stays in your house.
- **Extensible by design.** First-party modules and community plugins
  are built against the same plugin API. No special privileges for
  built-ins.
- **Working software over perfect plans.** Each milestone is used by
  the family before the next begins.

## Who It's For

Primary: A household with two parents and children at varying ages and
trust levels.
Extended: Any family that wants to run it — via a community plugin
marketplace.

## What It Is Not

- Not a cloud SaaS product
- Not a replacement for specialized tools (it integrates with them)
- Not feature-complete on day one
- Not built for a single age of child (it adapts)

## The Long Game

A platform where the family's needs drive the roadmap, community
plugins extend it, and the system becomes more valuable the longer
it runs.
